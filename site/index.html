Could a photo tell
these Usagi apart?

My master's schedule had no room for a computer vision course, so I started learning with my dolls. Could a model notice the small details that make each Usagi different?

I photographed seven dolls, compared image retrieval and classification, and fine tuned a vision model. The local app now identifies photos and shows its closest references.
