How this became a project

Could a photo tell
these Usagi apart?

I wanted to learn computer vision, but my master's schedule had no room for the course. My dolls gave me an idea: many share the same face, so could a model learn to notice the small things that make each one different?

I photographed seven dolls across separate sessions, tried image retrieval, trained a classifier, and fine tuned a vision model on my RTX 3060. The simplest retrieval baseline still tied the tuned model on the final known photo test.

This page is a look at my real collection. The local app also identifies uploaded photos and shows its closest references. You can explore the code, results, and visual notebooks in the project.
